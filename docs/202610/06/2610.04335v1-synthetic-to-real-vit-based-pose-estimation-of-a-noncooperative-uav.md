## 摘要
对非合作无人机（UAV）进行基于图像的远程位姿估计至关重要，因为无法事先对其施加影响或加装测量设备。基于深度学习的方法提供了有前景的解决方案；然而，其发展受限于获取带有精确位姿标签的大规模真实世界数据集的成本和难度。合成图像提供了替代方案，但在合成数据上训练的模型必须克服合成到真实的域差距，才能泛化到真实世界图像。本文研究了基于视觉Transformer（ViT）的单目无人机位姿估计模型固有的合成到真实泛化能力。所提方法采用自监督DINOv2骨干网络，仅在带标签的合成图像上训练，而在带标签的真实世界图像上评估。在训练和推理过程中引入了位姿歧义感知策略，以解决将三维目标投影到二维图像平面以及目标对称性所引起的歧义。推理阶段进一步集成了 $α$-$β$ 滤波器以改进位姿估计。为在运行需求下评估该模型，使用包含77,077张带标签无人机图像的真实世界数据集，在滤波前后分别以平均角度误差（MAE）和推理时间进行评估。滤波前，模型达到 $19.18^{\circ}$ 的MAE和 $13.25$ ms 的推理时间；滤波后，这两个值分别为 $8.74^{\circ}$ 和 $13.42$ ms。

## Abstract
Remote pose estimation of noncooperative Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) from imagery is critical, as they cannot be influenced or instrumented in advance. Deep-learning-based approaches offer a promising solution; however, their development is constrained by the cost and difficulty of acquiring large-scale real-world datasets with accurate pose labels. Synthetic imagery provides an alternative, but models trained on synthetic data must overcome the synthetic-to-real domain gap to generalize to real-world imagery. This work investigates the inherent synthetic-to-real generalization capability of a Vision Transformer (ViT)-based model for monocular UAV pose estimation. The proposed approach employs a self-supervised DINOv2 backbone and is trained exclusively on labeled synthetic imagery while being evaluated on labeled real-world imagery. Pose ambiguity-aware strategies are incorporated during training and inference to address ambiguities arising from the projection of a three-dimensional target onto a two-dimensional image plane and from target symmetries. An $α$-$β$ filter is further integrated during inference to improve pose estimations. To assess the model under operational requirements, it is evaluated in terms of Mean Angular Error (MAE) and inference time, both before and after filtering, using a real-world dataset containing 77,077 labeled UAV images. Before filtering, the model achieves an MAE of $19.18^{\circ}$ and an inference time of $13.25$ ms, whereas after filtering, these values are $8.74^{\circ}$ and $13.42$ ms, respectively.
